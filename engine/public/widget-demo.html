Careful when you click it on this page: the JSON-LD in the head outranks the table on the left, so you will get the £45 gig, not the £30.70 cinema trip. That ordering is the whole design — structured data first, scraping only when there is nothing else.

What it does with the number

Nothing, until you say so. The sheet shows the detection, its confidence, and which strategy produced it. You edit anything that is wrong, name who is paying, and only then does it POST /v1/groups — after which each person gets their own QR, approves their own share, and the cap is enforced at the card network rather than by this page.
