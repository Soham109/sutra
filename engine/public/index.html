sutra · GMP/1

Every payment protocol assumes one person is saying yes.

Groups aren't one person. GMP/1 is the multi-principal layer: N people, N cards, N network-enforced mandates, one atomic-enough commit. No pooled funds. No one fronts the money. Ever.
1Organizer creates a group with a cart and a commit policy.
2Each member passkey-approves a mandate on their own card — capped at their share, locked to the merchant, enforced by the card network.
3When the policy passes, the engine charges everyone in one window. If it can't, it cancels every mandate — nobody was ever charged.

Built on Prava mandates: approval is decoupled from charging, so humans consent on their own schedule and the engine commits in one tight window. The engine never sees a card number and never holds funds.
