# Final storyboard

## Visual grammar

- Warm off-white canvas, near-black type, white cards, muted orange accent.
- One main headline, one supporting visual system, one cause-and-effect beat.
- UI text stays large enough to understand on a paused 1080p frame.
- Chapter changes are clean cuts; internal elements provide the motion.
- Captions live in one stable lower pill and never cover the proof state.

## Eight frames that define the film

1. **Debt collector:** large “One card pays. Everyone else owes.” beside the
   phone, with the outstanding amount as the only statistic.
2. **Reversal:** four named approvals converge on `all_of(4)`; the visual
   explains the protocol before any architecture language appears.
3. **Consent:** four equal cards show exact share, cap, own-card boundary, and
   a visibly held approval state. The fourth yes flips the group to committed.
4. **Coordination:** four entry modes collapse into a real plan board; the
   ranking visibly reorders when Maya answers.
5. **Agent limit:** a normal group thread on the left, a compact capability
   checklist on the right, and the refusal connecting them.
6. **NANDA proof:** pooled credits and direct Prava mandates share the same
   geometry so the structural difference is obvious at a glance.
7. **Honest edge:** four colored cards cannot enter one card field; the lower
   row separates supported, at-venue, and unsupported checkout shapes.
8. **Receipt:** a large readable signed artifact, then only the closing line
   and URL.

## Motion beats

- Messages arrive, but Ada’s outstanding number does not change.
- Approval checks accumulate one at a time; charges do not.
- The last required approval changes all four outcomes together.
- A plan answer moves a venue and recalculates its score.
- The refusal appears only after the payment-shaped request.
- NANDA terminal checks arrive after the rail comparison is understood.
- The receipt fills entry by entry before resolving to the closing promise.
